# Fallout Chat Mod — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-06
**Version:** 1.0

This policy explains what the Fallout Chat Mod service (the **Service**) collects, why,
how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It covers the desktop overlay, the
website at `falloutchatmod.com`, the Discord bot, and the optional in-game HUD mods.

The Service is operated by the Fallout Chat Mod project maintainer (the **Developer**).
Contact details are published in the project repository.

---

## 1. What is collected

### 1.1 Account and identity

- **Discord ID, username, display name, and avatar URL** — obtained via Discord OAuth
  when you sign in. This is how your account is identified.
- **SteamID64** — obtained from Steam's server-verified OpenID response when you choose
  Steam sign-in or link Steam to the overlay/HUD account. Steam is an alternative provider;
  the Service does not receive your Steam password or payment details.
- **A *Fallout 76* character name that you supply yourself** during onboarding or in
  Settings. This is optional. If you do not supply one, your Discord display name is
  used in chat instead.
- **An optional FCM chat name** that you set from your Profile or with the Discord
  `/name` command. This is a free account setting; clearing it restores the ordinary
  Fallout 76 / Discord-derived name.
- **An install token** — a random identifier generated by the desktop client to
  associate your installation with your account.

The Service does **not** read your *Fallout 76* process memory, game files, or network
connections. It only checks whether the `Fallout76` process is running, in order to show
or hide the overlay. See `docs/TERMS.md` §1.1.

### 1.2 Chat content

- **Messages you send through the Service**, including the channel, timestamp, and
  author.
- **Moderation records** where applicable: reports you file or receive, and any mute,
  kick, or ban applied to your account, with the reason and issuing moderator.

### 1.3 Cosmetic settings

If you customise your chat appearance, the Service stores your chosen colour, effect,
and tag. Your optional free chat name is stored separately with your account.

### 1.4 Supporter subscriptions

If you subscribe, the Service stores **only** your Discord ID, the tier, and whether the
subscription is currently active.

**The Service never receives, stores, or processes your payment details.** Subscriptions
are handled entirely by Discord, which acts as merchant of record. Card numbers, billing
addresses, and tax information are held by Discord and its payment processor, never by
the Developer. See §3.

### 1.5 Operational data

Standard server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp, user agent) are produced for
security, abuse prevention, and debugging.

---

## 2. Why it is collected

| Purpose | Data used |
| --- | --- |
| Operating the chat service | Account identity, messages |
| Showing you the right name and appearance in chat | Identity, cosmetic settings |
| Moderating the community | Messages, moderation records, reports |
| Preventing abuse and securing the service | Operational logs, install token |
| Delivering supporter cosmetics you paid for | Discord ID, tier, subscription status |

The legal basis, where the GDPR applies, is **legitimate interest** for operating and
securing the Service, **contract** for delivering a subscription you purchased, and
**consent** for anything you optionally supply (such as a character name).

---

## 3. Third parties

Data is not sold, licensed, or shared for advertising. It is shared only with the
following processors, and only as needed to run the Service:

| Third party | What it receives | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discord** | Your Discord account identity; all payment data for subscriptions | Authentication, the chat bridge, and subscription billing as merchant of record |
| **OpenAI** | Message text submitted to the moderation endpoint | Automated content moderation. This is a moderation-only classification call. See `docs/moderation/ai-moderation.md`, which documents the kill switch and the exact scope. |

Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

---

## 4. Retention

- **Chat messages: up to 90 days**, then automatically purged.
- **Audit and moderation records:** retained while needed for community safety.
- **Account, cosmetic, and subscription records:** retained while your account exists.
- **Server logs:** short-lived, retained for operational and security purposes.

---

## 5. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can request:

- **Access** — a copy of the data held about you;
- **Correction** — of anything inaccurate;
- **Deletion** — of your account and associated data;
- **Objection or restriction** — to particular processing.

To make a request, open an issue on the project repository or use the contact form at
`https://falloutchatmod.com`. Requests are handled by the Developer directly.

Deleting your account removes your account record, your cosmetic settings, and your
subscription record. **Cancelling a subscription is a separate action taken through
Discord** — deleting your FCM account does not cancel Discord billing, and the Developer
cannot cancel it on your behalf.

Messages you have already sent may be retained within the 90-day window for moderation
integrity, and moderation records may be retained where community safety requires it.

## 6. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and is subject to Discord's own
minimum age requirements, which may be higher in your country.
